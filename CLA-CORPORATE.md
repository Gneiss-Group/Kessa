# Kessa Corporate Contributor License Agreement

Adapted from the Apache Software Foundation's Corporate Contributor License
Agreement v2.0, with every reference to the Foundation replaced by Gneiss Group
Inc. The Kessa-specific additions are identical to those in the individual
agreement: the preamble, the final sentence of section 2, and section 5's
treatment of AI assistance. The mechanism and logic are the same for a corporate
entity; the difference is that the corporation makes the representations on behalf
of its authorized employees.

**Who signs this.** An entity (the "Corporation") whose employees contribute to
Kessa, where the Corporation owns the intellectual property those employees
create. Each employee also signs the individual agreement,
[`CLA.md`](CLA.md). This one authorizes them to do so.

**How to sign.** Unlike the individual agreement, this is not signed through the
pull request bot. Contact Gneiss Group Inc. to execute it, and provide Schedule A
below. Contributions from listed employees are recognized once it is on file.

---

## About Kessa's licensing and this agreement

Kessa operates under a deliberate two-tier licensing model:

- **Protective tier (`AGPL-3.0-only`):** our core components (enforcement engine,
  proxy, issuer, agent). We also offer these components under a separate
  commercial license to organizations that cannot comply with the AGPL.
- **Permissive tier (`Apache-2.0`):** the independent verifier and our designated
  plug-point interfaces (such as `auditsink`, which carries a Section 7 additional
  permission).

**Why this is required.** First, to ensure clear provenance for all code in the
project. Second, because we offer the AGPL core commercially, we need explicit
permission to distribute contributions under those separate commercial terms.

**What this means for the Corporation.** The Corporation is not assigning
copyright to Gneiss Group Inc. and retains ownership of its original work. This
agreement grants a broad license to use, distribute, and commercially sub-license
those contributions, regardless of which tier the code lands in.
